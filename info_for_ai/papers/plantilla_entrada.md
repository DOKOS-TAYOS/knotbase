## Scope and traceability

This template records an identified scientific paper and its reading notes. It
is an entry catalogue template, not a research or peer-review-response
workflow. To find, verify, or analyze the paper before recording it, consult
[the research index](../research/indice_research.csv). To respond to reviewer
comments on an identified manuscript, use the
[peer-review response workflow](01_peer_review_response.md).

The template does not confirm bibliographic data and does not replace reading the paper, consulting primary sources, or preserving traceability. Record only supported information, distinguish notes from verified claims, and leave unchecked data pending.

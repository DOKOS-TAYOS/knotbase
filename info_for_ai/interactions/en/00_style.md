# Natural professional style in English

## Activation

Optional language reference. Do not load it by default for a normal interaction: the shared core and channel workflow already control tone, continuity, and brevity. Use it only when the request is specifically about English style/idiomaticity or a linguistic question remains unresolved by those rules.

## Writing style

Write idiomatic English rather than translating Spanish sentence patterns. Match forms of address and register to the recipient without defaulting to stiff or ceremonial phrasing.

In an existing exchange, continue naturally from the established context. For acknowledgements, acceptance, decline, delivery, or neutral replies, use direct wording proportionate to the situation rather than a fixed opening formula.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid formulaic, inflated, ceremonial, or literally translated courtesy language when it adds no information or does not fit the relationship and context. Prefer direct, idiomatic wording proportionate to the actual degree of formality.

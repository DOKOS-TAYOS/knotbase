# Replying to personal email in English

## Activation

Use this workflow when the send-ready reply from the user's personal email account must be written in English, regardless of the language of the email received.

## Approach

Keep the tone professional and friendly, but less institutional than a company email. It may be warmer when there is an existing relationship, while remaining clear and courteous.

Read the complete thread, identify the main and secondary requests, and address each one without restating the entire proposal. Include availability, material, or coordination details only when they help carry out the next step already chosen.

With people the user already knows, continue the conversation directly rather than restarting with first-contact language.

## Format and output

Include a subject only for a new email, not a reply in an existing thread. Use short paragraphs and a simple signature. Add no title, phone number, affiliation, or links unless necessary and documented. Return a send-ready email.

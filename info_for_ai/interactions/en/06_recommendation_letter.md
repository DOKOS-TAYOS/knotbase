# Recommendation letter in English

## Activation and evidence

Use this workflow when a recommendation letter on the user's behalf must be written in English. The letter should use facts and observations to explain why they recommend someone for a specific opportunity.

Identify, when available, the candidate, the opportunity, the nature and duration or context of the relationship, observed skills and results, concrete examples, evidence-based peer comparisons, and any institutional details needed for the signature. Do not invent any of these elements or substitute a summary of the candidate's résumé for evidence.

## Structure

1. State who is being recommended, for what, and how the user is able to assess them.
2. Develop two or three relevant strengths, supported where possible by projects, outcomes, or observable behavior.
3. Connect that evidence to the opportunity without repeating the same adjectives.
4. Close with a clear recommendation whose strength matches the available evidence.

Use a formal, personal, confident, and specific tone. Avoid generic superlatives, strings of adjectives, unsupported rankings or percentages, unobserved traits, invented anecdotes, promotional language, and automatic template openings.

## Missing information and output

If examples or facts are insufficient for a convincing letter, draft only from the available evidence and tell the user what specific information would strengthen it. Include a date, addressee, letterhead, or contact details only when supplied and relevant. Return a complete letter ready for review or signature.

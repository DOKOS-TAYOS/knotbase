# First professional contact in English

## Activation

Use this workflow when the send-ready first professional contact must be written in English, whether by email or direct message: propose a collaboration, request a paper or material, recommend someone, introduce an idea, or open a project conversation.

## Structure

1. Use a greeting suited to the relationship and channel.
2. State the specific reason for contacting the recipient.
3. Give only the context that explains why this person is relevant.
4. Make the request or proposal clear.
5. Close simply and without pressure.

Explain the genuine connection between the recipient's work and the proposal. Do not replace it with generic praise, the user's full biography, unsupported claims, or sales language. Explain the purpose before asking for a meeting and propose a proportionate next step.

When requesting a paper or material, identify it precisely and keep the request short; do not assume it can legally be shared. When recommending someone, explain their relevance using confirmed facts and frame the introduction as a suggestion, not an unsupported guarantee. For projects, provide enough context to decide whether to continue without imposing a call or agenda.

## Channel adaptation and output

On LinkedIn, minimize the introduction, greeting, and closing and omit a subject. In email, use a short informative subject and allow slightly more context; explain any relevant link or attachment in one sentence.

Return a send-ready message. If the channel cannot be inferred, write a concise email that can easily be shortened into a direct message.

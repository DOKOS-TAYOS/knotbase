# Replying to company email in English

## Activation

Use this workflow when the send-ready reply from the user's company email account must be written in English, regardless of the language of the email received.

## Institutional responsibility

Use a clear professional tone that is slightly more institutional than personal email without becoming bureaucratic. Distinguish the user's own view or authority from a commitment by the organization.

Identify whether the request affects other people, teams, resources, timelines, or decisions. Confirm only what is authorized and state the next step without assuming anyone else's availability. If internal coordination is required, say so without promising an outcome that has not been approved.

When declining, do not attribute the decision to the company, a team, or a named person unless that is documented. Do not offer unapproved alternatives or overexplain internal decisions.

## Confidentiality, format, and output

Do not include internal, contractual, financial, technical, or personal information unless necessary, and do not reproduce internal discussions to justify an external response.

Use a clear subject for a new thread, professional greeting and closing, short paragraphs, and only confirmed corporate details in the signature. Return a send-ready email that does not turn a personal response into an institutional commitment.

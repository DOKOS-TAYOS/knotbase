# Replying to LinkedIn messages in English

## Activation

Use this workflow when the send-ready LinkedIn reply must be written in English, regardless of the language of the message received.

## Process

1. Determine whether this is a first contact or an existing conversation and identify what the sender asks, proposes, or shares.
2. Decide whether to accept, decline, respond neutrally, or request one specific detail.
3. Keep only the context needed for the reply and use concise, professional conversational language.

If the agreed next step is to move to email or a meeting, state that directly. Do not repeat an email address the recipient already has. Provide contact details only when documented and necessary.

## Format and output

Use roughly two to six sentences in most cases, with no subject line or signature. Use a list only for several concrete items that are clearer when separated. Return one send-ready message unless the user asks for alternatives.

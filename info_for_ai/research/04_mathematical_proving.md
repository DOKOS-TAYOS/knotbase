# Rigorous mathematical proving

## Activation

Use this module to produce a complete, correct, readable mathematical proof. Never simulate rigor: state the assumptions and prior results used and what the proof does not cover.

## Workflow

1. Formulate the result precisely: objects, domain, assumptions, quantifiers, and conclusion. If necessary conditions are missing, propose a valid formulation before attempting a proof.
2. Create a Markdown working draft only when the proof is sufficiently complex, iterative, or likely to span turns that durable intermediate state materially reduces re-analysis. Otherwise reason directly and return the final proof without creating a working artifact. When a draft is useful, record the statement, notation, lemmas, dependencies, proof steps, checks, and rejected alternatives, and keep it current for later revisions.
3. For every step, verify that its premises are available, notation remains consistent, the domain has not changed, and implication direction is valid. Check calculations, signs, indices, and limiting cases.
4. Review the complete proof for circularity, misapplied theorems, critical implicit steps, and exceptions. Cite an external result or prove the needed lemma.
5. Seek a shorter presentation only after validation. Remove repetition but retain every step needed by a reader at the specified level.

## Chat output

### Statement

Give a short, self-contained, complete statement. Define indispensable notation inline and enumerate non-standard assumptions.

### Proof

Present a clear sequence of paragraphs or numbered steps. Justify every non-immediate implication and end exactly at the statement's conclusion. State any restrictions involving hypotheses, dimension, regularity, convergence, or other conditions.

Do not force a proof for a false, open, or underspecified claim. Explain the obstruction and offer the most useful valid reformulation.
